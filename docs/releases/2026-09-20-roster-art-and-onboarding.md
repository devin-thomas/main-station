### The UMVC3 Marvel half

This release first shipped at 395 of 410. Capcom's own high-resolution art post
(`news.capcomusa.com/lets/browse/new-ultimate-marvel-vs-capcom-3-high-res-character-art`) serves
the 25 Capcom-side renders and nothing else: its page body ends with `[Edited By Moderator]`, the
Marvel-side links are gone, and the bulk download it offered is a MegaUpload URL dead since 2012.
Ten of the missing characters took their Marvel vs. Capcom 2 render as a labelled cross-version
fallback; the other 15 kept the typographic fallback. marvel.com was evaluated and rejected — one
wide masthead per character as `og:image`, several live-action film stills, MODOK's a 1680×800
carousel banner, and Dormammu's showing no character at all.

**verified** — the gap closed the same day from `devin-thomas/fgc-assets`, branch `fgc-assets`
at `4707bd7`, whose `catalog/manifests/357190.json` records 48 UMVC3 entries as the game's own
portraits mirrored by the SuperCombo Wiki. All 25 Marvel-side rows now use those, which also
retires the ten cross-version fallbacks: no UMVC3 row borrows an MvC2 render any more, and the
only cross-version credits left in the catalog are Melee's 26.

**verified** — each of the 25 was refetched from its recorded `image_url` and its SHA-256 matched
the value in that manifest byte for byte before it was accepted. 25 of 25 matched; none was taken
on trust.

**verified** — reviewed by eye as a 50-portrait contact sheet: every name matches its portrait,
and the Capcom-Unity and SuperCombo halves are the same art style, so the roster reads as one set.

**verified** — the 25 carry the new `community-mirrored` usage basis rather than being filed as
publisher art. The wiki hosts the art as a reference work and grants no reuse licence of its own,
so `sourcePublisher` reads `Capcom / Marvel, via the SuperCombo Wiki`, the credit line names
Capcom and Marvel, and `reviewUrl` points at the wiki page rather than at a licence.
`202609200003` widens the database's `character_art_reuse_mode` check to admit the value.

**note** — that catalog tracks 48 UMVC3 entries against MainStation's 50. It carries Jill
Valentine and Shuma-Gorath, which Capcom's manual omits as DLC, and omits Ryu and Hsien-Ko, both
of whom are in the game. Both already had Capcom-side art here, so nothing was blocked.

## Guided onboarding

**verified** — `/welcome` is the only route to a new public profile. `SettingsPage` no longer
carries a creation form; the account-claim branch links to the flow. `RequireAccount` guards the
route and carries the original destination through sign-in.

**verified** — the gate. `onboardingReadiness` counts only mains that pass `validateLineup`, so a
half-filled team does not satisfy it. Covered by unit tests and by e2e:

| Behaviour | Case |
| --- | --- |
| No main, no route to creation | `a profile cannot be created until at least one main is claimed` |
| Removing the last main re-closes it | `removing the only claimed main closes the door on creation again` |
| Team needs every slot and the team option | `a team game holds the claim until every slot and the team option are set` |
| Kameo slot offers only Kameos | `a Kameo slot only ever offers Kameos` |
| Full team parks the roster | `a full team parks the roster until a slot is reopened` |
| A filled slot can be reopened and replaced | `a claimed slot can be reopened and replaced` |
| Any number of mains | `more than one main can be claimed before the profile is created` |
| Existing member is redirected out | `a member who already has a profile is not shown the guided flow` |
| Signed-out visitor must sign in | `signed-out visitors must sign in before the guided flow opens` |

**verified** — the payload that reaches `claim_profile_draft` carries the claimed mains as
`category: 'main'`, in claim order, with the handle from the identity step.

**verified** — the roster is art, not names. The Game step renders 39 portraits across 13 tiles;
the roster step renders one portrait per Character (31 for SF6), served from `/art/thumb/` and
decoded in the browser, asserted on the deployed origin. Portraits also carry the slot rail, the
claimed-mains strip, the Mainline, and the Game page roster ledger.

**verified** — `CharacterPortrait` falls back to the Character's name in the same frame when a row
has no art and when an image fails to load, so a grid never collapses around a gap.

## Rollback

The Worker rolls back on its own; see `docs/release-rollback.md`, which now records this version
and what an older build finds at `/art`.

Two forward migrations carry the art. `202609200002_full_roster_character_art` replaced the launch
ledger with 395 rows; `202609200003_complete_character_art` widens the reuse-mode check for
`community-mirrored` and rewrites the ledger at 410. Both delete and re-insert rather than patch,
because a changed source changes the `storage_path` or `asset_sha256`. Neither touches user data:
the table holds catalog rows only, and the browser reads art from its own bundled catalog rather
than from the database. Both are safe to re-run.
