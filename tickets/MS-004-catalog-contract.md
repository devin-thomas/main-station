# MS-004 — Versioned catalog contract

**Depends on:** MS-003

Define deterministic Game Version, Character, Selection Schema, summary provenance, and art-rights seed formats plus a validator that rejects partial or cross-version Teams.

**Acceptance:** all 13 founding Games have a schema; solo, ordered Team, assist, fuse, fighter/Kameo, and other required shapes have fixtures; every published summary/art row has auditable provenance.
